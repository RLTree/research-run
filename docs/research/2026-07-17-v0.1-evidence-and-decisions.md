## Product decision

Three plausible first products were compared:

1. **Evidence-ledger CLI and workspace.** A researcher records sources, claims,
   evidence relationships, experiments, and current limits in a deterministic
   local format.
2. **Agent-ready project scaffolder.** A generator installs research-oriented
   contracts, folders, and checks into a new repository.
3. **Autonomous research orchestrator.** An agent plans searches and experiments,
   updates state, and coordinates research workflows.

Choose option 1. It provides direct researcher value without an AI provider,
turns the strongest Nathan-project practices into a reusable product, and creates
the typed state that future scaffolding or agents would need. Option 2 without a
working state model would ship ceremony. Option 3 would add trust, integration,
and evaluation burdens before the underlying evidence authority is proven.

The primary user is one graduate researcher or research engineer maintaining a
small, changing body of evidence. The first job is not "manage all research." It
is: **show me what we currently know, why we think it, what failed or remains
ambiguous, and what deserves attention next.**

## Canonical domain boundary

The implementation may refine names, but it must preserve these semantic roles:

- `ProjectManifest`: format version, project identity, and declared roots.
- `SourceRecord`: stable identity, citation or locator, provenance, and bounded
  notes. A source is not evidence for every claim by association.
- `ClaimRecord`: stable identity, exact claim text, scope, and human ownership.
- `EvidenceLink`: a typed relationship from a source, experiment, or artifact to
  one claim with a stance such as supports, limits, contradicts, or context, plus
  the specific evidence used.
- `ExperimentReceipt`: question, method or protocol reference, observations,
  interpretation, limitations, outcome, and next move. Negative and ambiguous
  outcomes are ordinary valid records.
- `ArtifactPointer`: workspace-safe or external locator, description, provenance,
  and optional digest. It is not an instruction to copy raw data.
- `ReviewDecision`: explicit human decision and rationale that may change the
  workspace assessment of a claim. AI authorship remains provenance, never
  authority.

Claim assessment is derived, not freely written. At minimum, deterministic status
must distinguish unsupported, limited, supported, contradicted, and unreviewed
states. Evidence links, generated prose, projections, retry, or file order cannot
silently promote a claim. "Supported" means supported within the declared
workspace evidence and review scope, not scientifically true in general.

Canonical records are the authority. Human and JSON status, indexes, context
packets, receipts, and summaries are projections that can be rebuilt. Publication
must be atomic and workspace-confined; an ambiguous prior effect must be inspected
before retry.

## Acceptance and truth policy

The final task must map every requirement to current evidence. A source review
does not prove code. Code does not prove packaging. Unit tests do not prove the
public journey. A local binary does not prove GitHub or CI state. A pull request
does not prove release or adoption. A self-authored technical review may find
defects, but it does not prove external validation or real-world usefulness.

The product can claim v0.1 readiness only when the clean-clone journey and
representative valid, invalid, interruption, retry, path, symlink, malformed
input, unsupported-promotion, redaction, packaging, and secret-scan cases pass on
the integrated tree. Real-world usefulness and scientific impact remain withheld
until researchers use the tool on real projects and return evidence.

## 2026-07-20 inventory scalability baseline

Current merged source at `fe3466647e207c642a98e5c80f3f569b62b175df`
streams indexed bytes into SHA-256, but fixes inventory authority at 2,048
entries, 64 MiB per file, and 512 MiB total. A focused unit test passed while
asserting the exact 512 MiB boundary and rejecting its next byte. Source
inspection also found that only the root `.research-run/` directory is skipped;
a nested workspace's `.research-run/` state and project material are recursively
collected and hashed. No current canonical type can retain an artifact root
while truthfully marking its descendants uninspected.

This establishes a product-structure failure, not a scientific result. The
successor may support streamed policies whose explicit totals exceed 64 GiB,
but higher limits imply higher I/O, CPU, and elapsed time. Reference-only and
child-workspace declarations must preserve observed identity and supplied
metadata without representing descendant content as hashed, validated,
scientifically verified, or even inspected.

## 2026-07-20 inventory scalability implementation evidence

The successor is implemented in the durable linked worktree on
`codex/inventory-policy`. A versioned `InventoryPolicy` embeds `u64` limits in
each new inventory plan and snapshot. New workspaces default to 20,000 indexed
entries, 2 GiB per indexed file, and 8 GiB indexed aggregate; explicit policy
limits above 64 GiB are accepted. Existing policyless snapshots retain the
legacy 2,048-entry, 64 MiB-file, and 512 MiB aggregate limits until an explicit
policy is supplied on reconciliation. This is a deterministic migration rather
than a silent reinterpretation of prior authority.

The same policy declares exact `reference-only` and `child-workspace`
boundaries. A reference-only root contributes its normalized path,
classification, rationale, declared provenance or manifest reference, and a
root-only observation. Its descendants are not opened, recursively enumerated,
hashed, validated, or scientifically verified. A child boundary records the
child's manifest identity and latest available canonical inventory identity
without recursively inventorying child material. Missing, malformed,
overlapping, escaping, symlinked, unknown-version, and undeclared nested
boundaries fail before publication.

Fresh serialized verification passed with
`CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=1 CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0 CARGO_PROFILE_TEST_DEBUG=0`: 179
library tests, 56 product tests, `cargo clippy --locked --all-targets -- -D
warnings`, `cargo fmt --check`, and `scripts/check-standards`. Mutation testing
was not run under the explicit resource decision. The 32 MiB per-inventory
record limit and 64 MiB workspace snapshot limit remain safety caps on canonical
metadata/history, not on raw reference-only data; exceptionally long indexed
history can still require a later, separately authorized storage design.

This evidence proves bounded implementation behavior and canonical claim
language only. It does not validate referenced artifacts, establish scientific
truth, prove a ZNF385A migration, or establish thesis-workspace suitability in
an actual scientific project.
