# Review And Completion

Review effort follows the decision boundary:

- Critical protected boundaries always block when their current proof fails.
- Claim-bearing material integration, product, release, and completion
  boundaries receive one exhaustive independent issue-set review, one coherent
  repair, and one final exact-candidate signoff attempt.
- If that final attempt fails materially, do not start a third round. Redesign,
  split or narrow scope, withhold the claim, or defer only demonstrably
  nonblocking debt.
- Bounded reversible work receives focused deterministic checks and at most one
  targeted review. Non-material work receives owner review and deterministic
  checks only.
- The full Contract/Claim, Orchestration/Recovery, Security/Trust-Boundary, and
  Product/Simplicity persona team is reserved for the claim-bearing material
  boundaries named above; it is not required after each source increment.
- Re-review requires changed candidate bytes, a newly eligible claim surface, or
  a demonstrated contradiction. Byte-identical reruns and speculative hardening
  do not keep a closed review loop open.

Every completion report names the exact candidate, commands and surfaces checked,
security/performance implications, residual gaps, and claims still withheld.
The audit cadence and candidate-binding rules live in `STANDARD.md`.
