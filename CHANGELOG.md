# Changelog

All notable Research Run changes are recorded here. The project follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

### Added

- Offline, local-first source, claim, evidence, experiment, knowledge, and
  relationship records in deterministic JSON.
- Byte-preserving project retrofit, reconciliation, lossless v0.1 migration,
  bounded retrieval, portable handoffs, and explicit recovery.
- Owner-anchored Ed25519 review authority with detached SSHSIG verification and
  exact evidence-graph binding.
- Package, install, installed-journey, source, dependency, security, coverage,
  mutation, Product Fitness observation, and release-candidate proof surfaces.

### Security

- Workspace confinement, symlink rejection, bounded reads, serialized writes,
  atomic no-clobber publication, fail-closed recovery, and replay-resistant
  review authorization.

### Claim ceiling

- Validation proves ledger integrity, not scientific truth.
- Cryptographic verification proves configured-key control, not personhood.
- Product Fitness, continuance, scientific impact, and real-world usefulness
  remain withheld until their separate evidence contracts are satisfied.
